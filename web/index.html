OpenRamp

Testnet demo. Test money only: no real cedis or USDC move.

How it works

  1. You open an order. The seller's USDC is locked in a smart contract nobody controls.
  2. You pay the seller in cedis by mobile money on Paystack's page.
  3. A program in secure hardware checks with Paystack that you paid, and the contract releases the USDC to you.

If the seller's side can't be checked, the seller's bond pays you instead.

Each step is a NEAR transaction you confirm in your wallet. Keep about 0.5 testnet NEAR in it for network fees; most of it comes back.
